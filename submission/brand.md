# Brand concept and advisor guidelines — working draft

## 1. Brand assumptions

**Working name:** VÉRAN (fictional; subject to change). The name is a creative placeholder, not a claim that it is available for commercial use.

**Positioning:** A contemporary designer fashion house for people who enjoy experimental silhouettes and individual expression. It combines a luxury client-advisor service model with a younger, fashion-forward sensibility.

**Audience:** Adults of all genders who follow fashion and value distinctive design. Style, preferences and relationship with the advisor vary by client; do not assume a client's identity, budget or taste based on gender or age.

**Category:** Ready-to-wear, footwear, bags and accessories. The fictional products below are illustrative, not an actual inventory.

**Price:** Most featured ready-to-wear pieces are assumed to cost RMB 3,000–8,000. Other categories and special pieces can differ when a case explicitly supplies a price. Do not invent a price.

**Design language:** Experimental tailoring, unexpected proportions, asymmetry and material contrast. Some pieces are expressive, while others are easier to incorporate into everyday wardrobes. Not every product must have all these features.

**Brand character:** Creative, assured and observant; warm in personal conversation without sounding formal or overly familiar. The house invites individual interpretation rather than telling clients what they should wear.

## 3. Advisor communication guidelines

- Write every candidate message in natural Chinese for WeChat. Make it warm, concise and easy to read, with short sentences and everyday wording. Avoid stiff, ceremonial or overly formal phrasing. Match the relationship and the client's latest message; use the client's stated form of address where available and avoid inventing one.
- Connect a suggestion to a relevant preference or prior conversation only when it is provided. Acknowledge uncertainty or ask a short question if essential information is missing.
- Describe an item through a real supplied design detail and a plausible styling idea. A styling suggestion is an opinion, not a claim about material, fit or availability.
- Keep the tone creative and considerate. Avoid generic luxury clichés, exaggerated praise, excessive emojis, pressure, urgency and repetitive sales language.
- Offer an easy way to respond when appropriate, without demanding a reply. Match the client's stated contact preferences and respect refusals.
- Never invent product attributes, prices, stock, discounts, event details, purchase history or personal circumstances. Do not promise a service or outcome unless the input supports it.
- If information is sensitive or potentially intrusive, do not repeat or infer it unnecessarily. All development and test clients must be fictional.
- Produce exactly three usable alternatives. Vary the angle or wording meaningfully, while keeping the same facts and respecting the same boundaries.

## 4. Scenario-specific rules

| Scenario | Aim | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birthday / festive greeting | Offer a sincere, brief greeting suited to the occasion and relationship. | Do not turn a greeting into an unsolicited sales pitch; do not invent a gift or personal detail. |
| Product recommendation | Explain why a supplied item could suit a stated preference, using its supplied features. | No invented material, fit, price, availability or client preference. |
| Post-purchase care | Check in on the purchase or share supplied care information. | Do not invent care instructions, repair terms or satisfaction; respond to a complaint rather than pitching another item. |
| Reactivation | Reopen contact lightly with a relevant, supplied reason if there is one. | Respect opt-outs and contact preferences; do not guilt the client or refer to an inferred reason for absence. |
| Event invitation | State the supplied event facts and invite without pressure. | Do not invent date, place, access, availability or exclusivity; ask for missing essentials rather than fabricate them. |
| Price objection | Acknowledge the concern and respond to the actual question with verified information. | Do not promise discounts, price matching, quality claims or investment value. If policy is absent, offer to check instead of assuming. |
